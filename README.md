# Multi-Omics Evidence Links *ABC1K7* to Defense Release and Carbon Reallocation in Coconut Domestication

**Authors:** Ninghuan You†, Huangsheng Ling†, Jing Chen, Wenrao Li, Mengxing Huang, Yu Zhang, Hongxing Cao*, Chengxu Sun* (`suncx@catas.cn`, ORCID: `0009-0007-9841-9000`)  
**Affiliations:** Coconut Research Institute (CRI-CATAS), Henan University, Haikou Affiliated Hospital of Central South University Xiangya School of Medicine, Hainan University  
**Target Journal:** *Plant Biotechnology Journal* (PBJ)  
**Version:** PBJ V39.1 (2026-06-18)

## Highlights

- **Five-Layer Multi-Omics Integration:** Combines X-ray CT phenomics (7,686 DICOM slices, ~1,200 HU dynamic range), widely targeted & untargeted metabolomics, TMT proteomics, transcriptomics, and population genomics.
- **Defense-to-Growth Metabolic Reallocation:** Landrace CK invests in flavonoid glycoside chemical defense and thick shells (7.9 mm), whereas domesticated dwarf W5 redirects carbon toward nucleotides and amino acids (e.g., dIMP 34.6×, L-proline 4.1×) with accelerated endosperm development.
- **Quantitative Metric for Developmental Canalization:** Transcriptomic coefficient of variation (CV) across 34 core flavonoid enzymes captures tight canalization in CK at TP2 (CV = 0.172) versus artificial selection–driven decanalization in W5 (CV = 0.485, +182%) and W6 (CV = 0.579).
- ***ABC1K7* Selective Sweep:** Population genetics converges selection signals on Chr5:19.75–20.35 Mb harboring the *ABC1K7* Y→F mutation (π ratio peak = 28.7; CDI ROH carried by 76% of W5 vs. 59% of CK).

## Abstract

We integrated five complementary omics layers—CT phenomics, widely targeted and non-targeted metabolomics, TMT proteomics, and transcriptomics—to dissect fruit development differences between improved coconut dwarf cultivars (W5, W6) and the progenitor tall landrace (CK). A quantitative CT developmental clock spanning ~1,200 HU tracked four tissue compartments non-destructively. CK accumulated flavonoid glycosides with tightly canalized transcriptional regulation (TP2 core enzyme CV = 0.172), whereas W5 exhibited regulatory release (CV = 0.485) and redirected carbon to nucleotides and amino acids. Population genomics converged the strongest selective sweep (π ratio = 28.7) on an ancestral low-recombination haplotype block harboring the *ABC1K7* Y→F mutation (Chr5:19.75–20.35 Mb). Our findings demonstrate that directional selection on standing variation drove decanalization of flavonoid defense and reallocated carbon to growth and yield, establishing a replicable multi-omics framework for perennial crop domestication.
